# Parallel multi-fidelity expected improvement method for efficient global optimization

## 摘要

Multi-fidelity optimization augments a small number of expensive high-fidelity samples with a larger number of inexpensive low-fidelity samples to improve optimization performance. A central issue is how to adaptively assign high- and low-fidelity simulations during the optimization process. This work proposes a new infill criterion, Filter-GEI, which adds an adaptive filter function to generalized expected improvement and accounts for correlations between high- and low-fidelity models to balance local and global search. Considering parallel computing, Filter-GEI selects multiple high- and low-fidelity samples per iteration. Tests on five mathematical problems and a turbine-blade engineering problem demonstrate the algorithm’s effectiveness.
