## 摘要

With increasing turbine inlet temperatures and blade loading, advanced flow control and thermal management for turbine tips are critical. This article proposes a squealer-tip design approach based on free-form deformation (FFD). Compared with traditional winglet configurations, FFD-based tip design offers high freedom and smoothness. Driven by a large-variable optimization algorithm, an automatic aerothermal optimization framework is established, with turbine-stage efficiency as the primary objective and tip heat transfer as a constraint. The optimized FFD squealer tip increases stage efficiency by 0.42% and slightly reduces tip heat transfer. Analyses attribute the aerodynamic improvements to changes in the scraping vortex, tip-leakage vortex, upper-passage vortex, and their interactions. Reduced suction-side leakage lowers forward-region tip heat transfer, while full reattachment raises it in the center-rear region.
