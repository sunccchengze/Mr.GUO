## 摘要

The aerodynamic shape optimization of turbine blades is an expensive black-box problem. Inspired by transfer learning, the paper leverages samples from related completed tasks. A sample-weighted variational autoencoder includes source samples in training to raise the proportion of high-scoring candidates in the learned target design space and improve reparameterization accuracy for source samples. A multi-fidelity surrogate extracts information from the source samples to guide the search toward the target optimum. The resulting knowledge-transfer accelerated aerodynamic shape optimization procedure, KT-ASO, is tested on a highly loaded low-pressure turbine blade. Compared with procedures without knowledge transfer, it reduces at least 50% of function calls for similar optimum solutions and obtains better solutions under the same sample budget.
