## 摘要

A geometric convolutional neural network is trained on a low-geometrical-variability parametric representation of Rotor 37 to predict turbomachinery performance in seconds. Used in optimization, it yields a predicted 2.86% isentropic-efficiency increase, with CFD validation showing 1.81%. The achievable improvement is limited by the low-degree-of-freedom parameterization; a more complex representation and continuous-learning approach are introduced for broader design variations.
