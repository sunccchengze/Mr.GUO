## 摘要

Proper orthogonal decomposition is applied to large-eddy simulations of two high-loaded low-pressure turbine cascades under unsteady inflow to investigate entropy production. Integrated terms from the stagnation-pressure transport equation and POD separate coherent-flow contributions to turbulent-kinetic-energy production and dissipation, including wake-filament migration, bowing, tilting and reorientation, boundary-layer streak breakup, and wake-vortex formation. The analysis identifies dominant loss-generating modes and locations. An optimized profile with higher loading and lower global loss results; stronger early-passage acceleration reduces upstream wake-migration losses and earlier suction-side transition reduces trailing-edge-wake TKE production.
