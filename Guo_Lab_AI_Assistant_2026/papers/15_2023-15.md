## 摘要

Turbomachinery design optimization is an expensive black-box problem. Multi-fidelity optimization uses low-fidelity samples to assist limited high-fidelity samples, but sequential sampling can make multi-fidelity optimization worse than single-fidelity optimization in the final solution. The paper proposes multi-single-fidelity optimization (MSFO). DBSCAN identifies local areas where low-fidelity samples are no longer effective; a global multi-fidelity surrogate and local single-fidelity surrogates are combined to overcome limitations of conventional multi-fidelity procedures.
