## 摘要

A strategy couples artificial neural networks for complex unstructured-data regression with dimensionality-reduction algorithms, enabling global optimization in high-dimensional aerodynamic applications at reduced computational cost. It is demonstrated on efficiency optimization of a modern jet-engine fan blade under a pressure-ratio constraint. The proposed approach achieves improvements comparable to a state-of-the-art adjoint method, with reduced computational cost and better scalability toward multi-objective optimization.
