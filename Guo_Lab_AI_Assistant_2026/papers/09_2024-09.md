## 摘要

In this study, a physics-enhanced neural operator framework is proposed to enhance generalization prediction ability for turbine endwall cooling layouts with variable numbers of film holes. Inspired by the film-cooling superposition principle, the superposition-based deep neural operator (SDNO) divides temperature-field prediction into two stages. First, the cooling layout is divided into sub-parts and a transformer-based neural operator, Calculate Net, predicts each sub-part temperature field. Second, Superposition Net combines those fields to obtain the full cooling-layout field. A signed-distance function sensitive to cooling-hole locations preprocesses the input rather than using binary pixel layout data. Training uses layouts with one to five holes at different locations; the trained model accurately predicts film effectiveness for unseen layouts with 10–20 holes and exhibits improved accuracy. The results demonstrate SDNO effectiveness.
