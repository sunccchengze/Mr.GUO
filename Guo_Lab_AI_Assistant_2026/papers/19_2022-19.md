## 摘要

Machine learning is applied to forced-response prediction for a gas-turbine compressor blade. Conventional harmonic or time-domain CFD computes forcing and aero damping to calculate high-cycle-fatigue reserve factors, often requiring hundreds of calculations. A neural network trained on historical data directly predicts reserve factor with high confidence from finite-element predicted parameters, enabling earlier aero-mechanical assessment without high-fidelity CFD. A convolutional neural network for image recognition also assists identification of FE-predicted mode shapes and improves reserve-factor predictions.
