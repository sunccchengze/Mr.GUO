## 摘要

CFD-based turbomachinery-cascade optimization is a high-dimensional expensive black-box problem: multistage configurations can exceed 100 variables while only hundreds or thousands of CFD simulations are affordable. The paper proposes generalized surrogate-assisted differential evolution (GSDE), incorporating a radial basis function in population regeneration to improve local exploitation while balancing global and local search. On 50–100-dimensional benchmarks, GSDE obtains the optimum with fewer than 1000 calls. For a 126-variable 3.5-stage compressor, 1500 CFD evaluations raise total-to-total efficiency by 1.104%; conventional differential evolution needs nearly 20,000 evaluations for comparable improvement.
