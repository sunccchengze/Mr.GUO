## 摘要

To solve turbine design optimization problems efficiently, surrogate-based optimization algorithms are frequently used. To further reduce turbine-design cost, multi-fidelity-surrogate-based optimization augments a small number of expensive high-fidelity samples with many cheap low-fidelity samples. However, the low-fidelity samples can become misleading as the number of high-fidelity samples grows. To address this issue, an ensemble weighted multi-fidelity surrogate is proposed: density-based spatial clustering detects regions in which the multi-fidelity surrogate is less accurate, a local single-fidelity surrogate is built there, and the two surrogates are combined with adaptive weights to guide optimization. The resulting algorithm is termed multi- and single-fidelity surrogate fused optimization.
