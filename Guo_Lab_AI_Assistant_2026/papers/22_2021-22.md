## 摘要

Expected-improvement Bayesian optimization can converge suboptimally through both over-exploitation and over-exploration—querying points of high uncertainty but poor objective value. The paper adaptively calibrates the incumbent rather than fixing it at the present best solution, and introduces calibrated EI and recalibrated EI with distance constraints for local exploitation and global exploration. The CR-EI algorithm is validated on seven benchmark functions and an airfoil optimization problem.
