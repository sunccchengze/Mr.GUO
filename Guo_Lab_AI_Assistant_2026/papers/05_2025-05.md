## 摘要

The traditional design of pump-jet propulsor (PJP) usually relies on costly and time-consuming experiments and simulations. Recently, the digital revolutions have driven the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and the fast modeling of PJP. To this end, this paper proposes an AI-assisted fluid-structure modeling and optimization method for the PJP. Firstly, the geometric parametric modeling of the PJP is developed, and the related performance characteristics are obtained via simulation methods. Secondly, based on the generated data, Gaussian process regression (GPR) is used to model hydrodynamic performance, proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) is employed to predict deformed blade coordinates, and a novel graph neural network (GNN) is proposed to handle the nonlinear blade stress field. Finally, multi-objective evolutionary algorithms are performed for PJP optimization with POD modal coefficients as design variables. Leveraging AI, the study constructs a fluid-structure digital twin of the PJP, facilitating rapid performance analysis and optimization.
