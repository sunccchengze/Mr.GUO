## 摘要

Analysis, optimization, and uncertainty quantification of turbomachinery aerodynamics require compute-intensive CFD. This work investigates graph neural networks as surrogate models, including for multi-fidelity use, to estimate flow quantities while preserving geometric accuracy at a fraction of classical CFD effort. A multiscale graph neural network addresses the large information distances of message-passing graph blocks on large meshes. Training uses a space-filling design of experiments with 100 CFD solutions across geometries. One use case predicts complete fluid-domain flow quantities on a 2.5-million-point mesh; another predicts scalar quantities such as pressure or temperature on surface meshes up to 30,000 points.
