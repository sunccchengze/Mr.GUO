## 摘要

Many real-world problems, such as airfoil design, involve optimizing a black-box expensive objective function over complex-structured input spaces, such as discrete or non-Euclidean spaces. Mapping the input into a latent space yields a generative-model-based optimization procedure, but the latent dimension is hard to determine and can create a trade-off between solution accuracy and convergence rate. This paper proposes generative multiform optimization, which optimizes over multiple latent spaces simultaneously. A generative model promotes positive correlation between latent spaces to facilitate knowledge transfer, while Bayesian optimization exchanges information among them continuously. Experiments on airfoil and corbel design and an area-maximization problem show convergence to better designs under a limited computational budget.
