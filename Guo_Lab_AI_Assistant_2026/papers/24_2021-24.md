## 摘要

A novel non-axisymmetric endwall is shaped near the blade leading edge and between adjacent blades for a large-turning-angle gas turbine with a purge slot. Three separate optimizations use aerodynamic loss, film-cooling effectiveness, and heat-transfer coefficient as objectives. The solution minimizing heat-transfer coefficient provides the most desirable balance, reducing aerodynamic loss and endwall thermal load by suppressing horseshoe vortex and cross flows. Flow-thermal analysis yields design guidance for non-axisymmetric endwalls.
