### 1. 贝叶斯优化、高维代理模型与高效全局优化（Surrogate & Bayesian Optimization）
- 攻克了贝叶斯优化中经典 EI 准则在复杂多峰问题中的“过度探索（Over-exploration）”与“过度开发（Over-exploitation）”失衡难题，提出了自适应校准机制（CR-EI）。
- 建立了基于 Filter-GEI 和 EMFS 的多保真度自适应采样策略，突破了低保真模型在优化后期误导搜索方向的瓶颈。
- 开创了面向 100 维以上高维昂贵黑箱优化（HEB）的代理模型辅助差分进化算法（GSDE）与动态子空间聚合算法（DA-EGO），成功应用于 3.5 级压气机（126 变量）与多级透平叶栅一体化优化。

### 2. 生成式深度学习与知识迁移优化（Generative Models & Transfer Optimization）
- 创新性地将变分自编码器（VAE）与生成对抗网络（GAN）引入复杂气动几何参数化与设计空间建模，实现从高维非欧几里得几何到低维连续潜在空间的保形映射。
- 提出了基于样本加权（Sample-Weighted）与梯度重参数化的知识迁移机制（GTO / SW-VAE），打破了“从零开始优化”的传统范式，将历史高性能设计经验高效迁移到全新涡轮叶片设计中。

### 3. 物理增强神经网络算子与智能全景预测（Physics-Enhanced Neural Operators & AI4Turbomachinery）
- 提出直接预测满足 Navier-Stokes 控制方程的基础物理场（温度、压力、密度、速度），再解析积分获得叶栅子午面性能的全景预测新范式（TNO）。
- 结合流体力学气膜冷却叠加原理与符号距离函数（SDF），构建了叠加深层神经算子（SDNO），实现了仅用 1~5 孔训练集精准泛化预测 10~20 复杂孔排温度场的卓越外推能力。
- 引入可解释机器学习 SHAP 方法对航空发动机高压透平第一级 93 维复杂设计空间进行特征归因与深度数据挖掘。

### 4. 复杂流动控制、气动热耦合与跨声速风洞实验（Aero-Thermal Design & Flow Physics）
- 针对高负荷涡轮端壁与叶顶严重的三维二次流与热负荷，提出了新型非轴对称端壁造型（NAE）与基于自由变形技术（FFD）的叶尖气动热优化方法。
- 开展跨声速环形叶栅风洞气动实验与油流显示验证：在出口马赫数 0.6~0.95 范围内测量，出口马赫数 0.8 时非轴对称端壁使面平均总压损失系数降低 14.0%，油流显示通道内流线更贴近压力面、通道涡发展受抑制［出版商页面 摘要，论文 09］。

### 方向一：贝叶斯优化、高维代理模型与高效全局优化算法

#### 1. 【论文 01】Calibrated and recalibrated expected improvements for Bayesian optimization
- **中译题名：** 面向贝叶斯优化的校准与重校准期望改进算法
- **作者团队：** Zhendong Guo, Yew-Soon Ong, Haitao Liu
- **刊物卷期：** *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization*, 2021, Vol. 64, pp. 3549–3567
- **DOI：** [10.1007/s00158-021-03038-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-021-03038-3)
- **本地文件：** `郭老师论文/s00158-021-03038-3.pdf`
- **摘要原文（Abstract, verbatim，逐字摘录见 `corpus/facts/P01.md` §二）：**
  > Expected improvement (EI), a function of prediction uncertainty σ(x) and improvement quantity (ξ − ŷ(x)), has been widely used to guide the Bayesian optimization (BO). However, the EI-based BO can get stuck in sub-optimal solutions even with a large number of samples. The previous studies attribute such sub-optimal convergence problem to the "over-exploitation" of EI. Differently, we argue that, in addition to the "over-exploitation", EI can also get trapped in querying samples with maximum σ(x) but poor objective function value y(x). We call such issue as "over-exploration", which can be a more challenging problem that leads to the sub-optimal convergence rate of BO. To address the issues of "over-exploration" and "over-exploitation" simultaneously, we propose to calibrate the incumbent ξ adaptively instead of fixing it as the present best solution in the EI formulation. Furthermore, we propose two calibrated versions of EI, namely calibrated EI (CEI) and recalibrated EI (REI), which combine the calibrated incumbent ξ_Calibrated with distance constraint to enhance the local exploitation and global exploration of promising areas, respectively. After that, we integrate EI with CEI & REI to devise a novel BO algorithm named as CR-EI. Through tests on seven benchmark functions and an engineering problem of airfoil optimization, the effectiveness of CR-EI has been well demonstrated.
- **中文深度解析与创新亮点：**
  - **研究痛点：** 经典 EI 采集函数在迭代后期容易陷入次优解，过去文献普遍归咎于“局部过度开发”，而本文首次明确指出“高不确定性区域的过度探索（Over-exploration）”同样会导致采样浪费和收敛停滞。
  - **核心方法：** 提出自适应校准当前最佳解 $\xi$ 的策略，构造校准期望改进（CEI）与重校准期望改进（REI），并融合为 CR-EI 算法，自适应权衡探索与开发。
  - **验证结论：** 在 7 个数学基准函数与一个翼型气动优化问题上，与 EGO、E3I、GEI、PEI 四种 EI 类算法对比［原文 P01 §4.1］：除 Trid10 外，CR-EI 在其余 6 个基准上以更快收敛速率取得最优解［原文 P01 §4.3］；翼型算例目标值均值 −246.34（EGO −239.34）［原文 P01 Table 2］。

#### 2. 【论文 02】Parallel multi-fidelity expected improvement method for efficient global optimization
- **中译题名：** 面向高效全局优化的并行多保真度期望改进方法
- **作者团队：** Zhendong Guo, Qineng Wang, Liming Song, Jun Li
- **刊物卷期：** *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization*, 2021, Vol. 64, pp. 1457–1468
- **DOI：** [10.1007/s00158-021-02931-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-021-02931-1)
- **本地文件：** `郭老师论文/s00158-021-02931-1.pdf`
- **摘要原文（Abstract, verbatim，逐字摘录见 `corpus/facts/P02.md` §二）：**
  > Multi-fidelity optimization (MFO) has received extensive attentions in engineering design, which resorts to augmenting the small number of expensive high-fidelity (HF) samples by a large number of low-fidelity (LF) but cheap samples to improve the optimization performance. A key factor that influences the effectiveness of MFO is how to adaptively assign samples for HF and LF simulations in the iteration process. To address such sample assignment issue in MFO, we propose a new infill criterion named Filter-GEI, which imposes an adaptive filter function on top of the generalized expected improvement (GEI) acquisition function. In particular, by taking the correlations between HF and LF models into account, the Filter-GEI can efficiently allocate HF and LF samples to achieve a good balance in between the local and global search. Furthermore, considering parallel computing, the Filter-GEI infills multiple HF and LF samples in each iteration, which can further improve its efficiency as computing power increases. Through tests on five mathematical toy problems and one engineering problem for the turbine blade design, the effectiveness of the proposed algorithm has been well demonstrated.
- **中文深度解析与创新亮点：**
  - **核心方法：** 针对多保真度优化中“高低保真度样本如何动态合理分配”的核心难题，提出了 Filter-GEI 采集准则，引入自适应滤波机制并综合高低保真模型相关性。
  - **工程价值：** 支持每轮迭代并行注入多个高、低保真评估样本以利用并行算力；在 GE-E3 涡轮静叶算例中，低保真网格（12,141 单元）与高保真网格（100,701 单元）的成本比约为 0.1［原文 P02 §4.3］。

#### 3. 【论文 07】An efficient surrogate-assisted differential evolution algorithm for turbomachinery cascades optimization with more than 100 variables
- **中译题名：** 面向超100变量叶轮机械叶栅优化的高效代理模型辅助差分进化算法
- **作者团队：** Zhendong Guo, Zijun Zhang, Yun Chen, Guangjian Ma, Liming Song*, Jun Li, Zhenping Feng
- **刊物卷期：** *Aerospace Science and Technology*, 2023, Vol. 142, Part B, 108675
- **DOI：** [10.1016/j.ast.2023.108675](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ast.2023.108675)
- **本地文件：** `郭老师论文/1-s2.0-S1270963823005710-main.pdf`
- **摘要原文（Abstract, verbatim，逐字摘录见 `corpus/facts/P07.md` §二）：**
  > The CFD-based design optimization of turbomachinery cascades is a typical high-dimensional expensive black-box (HEB) problem. Specifically, to fully consider the interactions between vanes and stages and thus achieving desirable solutions, the cascades of two or more stages have to be optimized simultaneously. The number of variables can reach to more than 100 while the affordable CFD simulations within a design cycle can be only a few hundred or thousand. Hence, how to achieve the optimal design of turbomachinery cascades on budget is very challenging. To solve this problem, a generalized surrogate-assisted differential evolution (DE) algorithm is proposed, which is labeled as GSDE. In GSDE, radial basis function is incorporated into the population regeneration process, which helps to enhance the local exploitation in each iteration and thus achieving a good balance in between global and local search. By validating on benchmark functions ranging from 50 to 100 dimensions, the proposed GSDE algorithm is observed to obtain the true optimal solution with less than 1000 function calls and have far better convergence rate than other state-of-the-art algorithms.
