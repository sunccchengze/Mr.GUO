## 1. 【论文 13】A Novel Multi-Fidelity Surrogate for Efficient Turbine Design Optimization

- **作者：** Qineng Wang, Liming Song, Zhendong Guo, Jun Li, Zhenping Feng（Crossref）
- **期刊：** Journal of Turbomachinery
- **年份：** 2024（Online: 2023-12-21）
- **卷期页码：** 146(4), 041011
- **论文编号：** TURBO-23-1281
- **DOI：** [10.1115/1.4064228](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4064228)
- **ASME 页面：** [打开论文页面](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/turbomachinery/article/146/4/041011/1184096/A-Novel-Multi-Fidelity-Surrogate-for-Efficient)
- **Google Scholar：** [检索标题](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22A+Novel+Multi-Fidelity+Surrogate+for+Efficient+Turbine+Design+Optimization%22)

### Abstract

Surrogate-based optimization algorithms are frequently used to solve turbine design optimization problems efficiently. Multi-fidelity-surrogate-based optimization augments a small number of expensive high-fidelity samples with a large number of inexpensive low-fidelity samples. However, although this strategy may improve convergence during the early stage, the low-fidelity samples can negatively affect the search as the number of high-fidelity samples increases, potentially producing a worse final solution than a single-fidelity surrogate based only on high-fidelity samples.

To address this problem, the paper proposes an **ensemble weighted multi-fidelity surrogate（EMFS）**. Density-based spatial clustering of applications with noise（DBSCAN）is first used to identify regions where the multi-fidelity surrogate cannot construct an accurate model. A local single-fidelity surrogate is then built in those regions. The EMFS combines the multi-fidelity and single-fidelity surrogates with adaptive weights to guide the optimization process. The resulting algorithm is called **multi- and single-fidelity surrogate fused optimization（MSFO）**.

### 中文摘要整理

论文提出 EMFS 和 MSFO，用于解决多保真代理模型在优化后期可能被低保真数据误导的问题。其核心做法是识别多保真模型表现不佳的局部区域，在这些区域使用高保真样本建立单保真代理模型，再通过自适应权重融合两类代理模型。

## 2. 【论文 04】Uncertainty Quantification of Aero-Thermal Performance of a Blade Endwall Considering Slot Geometry Deviation and Mainstream Fluctuation

- **作者：** Zhi Tao, Zhendong Guo, Liming Song, Jun Li（Crossref）
- **期刊：** Journal of Turbomachinery
- **年份：** 2021
- **卷期页码：** 143(11), 111013
- **论文编号：** TURBO-20-1425
- **DOI：** [10.1115/1.4051416](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4051416)
- **ASME 页面：** [打开论文页面](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/turbomachinery/article-abstract/143/11/111013/1110716/Uncertainty-Quantification-of-Aero-Thermal?redirectedFrom=fulltext)
- **Google Scholar：** [检索标题](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Uncertainty+Quantification+of+Aero-Thermal+Performance+of+a+Blade+Endwall+Considering+Slot+Geometry+Deviation+and+Mainstream+Fluctuation%22)

### Abstract

The paper proposes an efficient uncertainty quantification method for expensive computational fluid dynamics problems based on a Kriging surrogate. The method is used to quantify the effects of slot geometry deviations, including slot width and endwall misalignment, as well as fluctuations in mainstream conditions, including turbulence intensity and inlet flow angle, on the aero-thermal performance of turbine endwalls.

The results show that the actual endwall performance has a high probability of deviating from its nominal value. The maximum deviations of aerodynamic loss, area-averaged film-cooling effectiveness, and area-averaged Nusselt number reach **0.33%, 45%, and 5.0%**, respectively. The most sensitive regions associated with input uncertainties are also identified. A global sensitivity analysis shows that the inlet flow angle is the most significant parameter among the four uncertain inputs. In addition, a positive incidence angle is found to be detrimental to both the aerodynamic performance and thermal management of the endwall.

### 中文摘要整理

论文使用 Kriging 代理模型分析端壁槽几何偏差和主流条件波动对气动热性能的影响。结果显示，膜冷却有效度对不确定性尤其敏感，最大偏差可达 45%［原文 P04 摘要，见上引］；在四个不确定参数中，入口流动角是影响最大的因素，正攻角会同时损害端壁气动性能和热管理性能。

## 3. 【论文 14】Generative Model Based Parameterization for More Efficient Aerodynamic Optimization of Non-Axisymmetric Endwall

- **类型：** 会议论文（ASME Turbo Expo 2024, GT2024, London, 2024-06-24～28）
- **年份：** 2024（在线 2024-08-28）
- **会议/出版信息：** Volume 12D: Turbomachinery — Multidisciplinary Design Approaches, Optimization, and Uncertainty Quantification; …；论文号 GT2024-128792，文章号 V12DT34A025
- **作者：** Cunxi Li, Liming Song, Zhendong Guo, Zhao Yang, Jun Li, Zhenping Feng（Crossref）
- **DOI：** [10.1115/GT2024-128792](https://doi.org/10.1115/GT2024-128792)
- **ASME 页面：** [打开论文页面](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/GT/proceedings/GT2024/88087/V12DT34A025/1204815)
- **Google Scholar：** [检索标题](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Generative+Model+Based+Parameterization+for+More+Efficient+Aerodynamic+Optimization+of+Non-Axisymmetric+Endwall%22)

### Abstract（出版商摘要，逐字）

The Non-axisymmetric Endwall Contouring (NEC) technique has been proven effective in reducing the secondary losses of turbine cascades. However, the parameterization of NEC, especially for spline-based techniques, would face a tradeoff between issues such as the dimensionality of the design space, sample variability, and the proportion of abnormal shapes. To address the above issue and achieve a more efficient NEC optimization, a generative model based parameterization method is proposed. Specifically, to build a comprehensive design space that contains sufficient variability of NEC shapes, the samples of a set of conventional classical NEC parameterization methods are collected, which are combined by representing them with scatter coordinates in a uniform format. Then, to reduce the number of variables to the minimum extent, the Variational Autoencoder (VAE) is used to learn the intrinsic dimensions of the parameterized NEC design space. To ensure the smoothness of the contours and avoid abnormal shapes, a layer of NURBS is incorporated into the neural network architecture of VAE. This proposed model was used for the NEC parameterization of a high-pressure turbine stage testcase with 9 variables. Additionally, the proposed VAE was used for aerodynamic optimization of the non-axisymmetric endwalls, compared to other NEC parameterization approaches. The results demonstrate that the generative model based parameterization method can optimize aerodynamic performance more effectively while maintaining a lower limit on the number of performance evaluations. The effectiveness of our proposed generative model based parameterization method is well demonstrated.

### 中文摘要整理

论文针对样条类非轴对称端壁参数化在设计空间维度、样本多样性与畸形几何比例之间的权衡，收集多种经典 NEC 参数化方法的样本并统一为散点坐标，用变分自编码器（VAE）学习其内在维度（高压涡轮级算例为 9 个变量），并在 VAE 网络中嵌入 NURBS 层以保证型面光顺。与其他 NEC 参数化方法对比，该方法能在更低的性能评估次数上限内更有效地优化气动性能（摘要仅给定性结论，无具体百分比）。

## 4. 【论文 05】Effects of Cooling Configurations on the Aerothermal Performance of a Turbine Endwall With Jet Impingement and Film Cooling

- **作者：** Hongyan Bu, Zhendong Guo, Liming Song, Jun Li（ASME 页面脚注：Bu 与 Guo 并列第一作者）
- **期刊：** Journal of Turbomachinery
- **年份：** 2021
- **卷期页码：** 143(6), 061013
- **论文编号：** TURBO-20-1334
- **DOI：** [10.1115/1.4050358](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4050358)
- **ASME 页面：** [打开论文页面](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/turbomachinery/article-abstract/143/6/061013/1101648/Effects-of-Cooling-Configurations-on-the)
- **Google Scholar：** [检索标题](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Effects+of+Cooling+Configurations+on+the+Aerothermal+Performance+of+a+Turbine+Endwall+With+Jet+Impingement+and+Film+Cooling%22)

### Abstract

As turbine inlet temperatures increase, turbine components, especially endwalls, operate in increasingly harsh environments and require effective cooling. This paper establishes and validates a numerical model of a turbine endwall with both internal jet impingement cooling and external film cooling. Four geometric parameters are investigated: impingement-hole pitch, impingement-hole diameter, impingement-channel height, and film-hole diameter.

A uniform design method is used to generate a database representing the design space. The performance criteria include area-averaged overall cooling effectiveness, the standard deviation of overall cooling effectiveness, and the total pressure-drop coefficient of the cooling system. A Kriging model is built from the database, and analysis of variance is used to investigate the main effects and interactions among the design parameters. Designs with particularly good or poor performance are then selected for detailed analysis of flow and heat-transfer mechanisms.

### 中文摘要整理

论文研究射流冲击冷却与外部气膜冷却组合方案对涡轮端壁气动热性能的影响。研究考察冲击孔间距、冲击孔直径、冲击通道高度和气膜孔直径四个参数，并使用均匀设计、Kriging 代理模型和 ANOVA 分析设计空间。最终从参数主效应和交互作用出发，解释不同冷却构型对综合冷却效果、均匀性和压降的影响。

## 5. 【论文 20】Physics-enhanced AI model for panoramic prediction of the meridional performance of turbomachinery cascades

- **类型：** SPIE 会议论文——*Proc. SPIE* 14253, Second International Conference on Hydromechatronics and Advanced Robot Control Technology (HARCT 2026, Shaoguan, 2026-03-27～29)
- **年份：** 2026（出版 2026-07-07）；文章号 142530E
- **作者：** Cunzhong Gong, Zhendong Guo, Zhongcheng Liu, Liming Song（Crossref / SPIE）
- **DOI：** [10.1117/12.3117536](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3117536)
- **SPIE 页面：** [打开论文页面](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/14253/3117536/Physics-enhanced-AI-model-for-panoramic-prediction-of-the-meridional/10.1117/12.3117536.full)
- **Google Scholar：** [检索标题](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Physics-enhanced+AI+model+for+panoramic+prediction+of+the+meridional+performance+of+turbomachinery+cascades%22)

### Abstract（出版商摘要，逐字）

Traditional CFD and 1D/quasi-3D through-flow programs suffer from bottlenecks of "being slow" and "being inaccurate" in turbomachinery design. The fourth paradigm of AI offers a new direction, yet purely data-driven models face issues such as limited samples and poor generalization. To solve the above problems, this paper proposes a panoramic prediction model based on physics-informed machine learning, taking a high-pressure turbine stage as the research object which includes the features of the Navier–Stokes equations. The model first predicts basic variables (temperature, pressure, density, velocity) and then comprehensively evaluates meridional-plane performance parameters. A dataset is constructed using the GE-E3 high-pressure stage, and a Transformer-augmented neural operator network is employed for model building and testing. It shows that the model can not only predict overall performance indicators (e.g., power, efficiency, mass flow rate) but also spanwise distributions of key parameters (e.g., exit flow angle, stage reaction) at the millisecond level, with relative errors of all parameters below 1%, verifying the effectiveness of the proposed physics-informed flow field prediction model.

### 中文摘要整理

论文以 GE-E3 高压涡轮级为对象，提出包含 N-S 方程特征的物理信息全景预测模型：先预测温度、压力、密度、速度基本场，再综合评估子午面性能参数；采用 Transformer 增强的神经算子网络。模型可在毫秒级给出总体指标（功率、效率、流量）与关键参数的展向分布（出口气流角、级反动度），所有参数相对误差低于 1%［出版商页面 摘要，见上引］。
