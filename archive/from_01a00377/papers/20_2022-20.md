## 摘要

Complex-structured black-box design spaces can be mapped to latent spaces for generative-model-based optimization, but choosing latent dimension trades solution accuracy against convergence rate. Generative multiform optimization runs optimization in multiple latent spaces simultaneously; a generative model promotes positive correlation among latent spaces for knowledge transfer, and Bayesian optimization exchanges information continuously between them. Airfoil, corbel-design, and area-maximization experiments demonstrate improved designs under limited computational budget.
