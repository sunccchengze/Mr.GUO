## 摘要

Multi-fidelity optimization augments scarce expensive high-fidelity samples with cheap low-fidelity samples. Filter-GEI adds an adaptive filter to generalized expected improvement, accounts for high/low-fidelity correlation to assign samples adaptively, and supports parallel infill of multiple high- and low-fidelity samples per iteration. Tests on five mathematical problems and a turbine-blade engineering problem demonstrate its effectiveness.
