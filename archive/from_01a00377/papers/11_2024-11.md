## 摘要

To solve turbine design optimization problems efficiently, surrogate-based optimization is frequently used. Multi-fidelity-surrogate optimization augments a small number of expensive high-fidelity samples with many cheap low-fidelity samples; however, low-fidelity samples can become misleading as high-fidelity samples accumulate. The paper proposes an ensemble weighted multi-fidelity surrogate. Density-based spatial clustering detects regions where multi-fidelity modeling is less accurate, a local single-fidelity surrogate is built in those regions, and adaptive weights combine the multi- and single-fidelity surrogates to guide the optimization. The related algorithm is called multi- and single-fidelity surrogate fused optimization.
