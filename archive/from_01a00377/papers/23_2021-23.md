## 摘要

A Kriging-surrogate uncertainty-quantification method is proposed for expensive CFD. It quantifies the effects of slot-width and endwall-misalignment deviations and turbulence-intensity and inlet-flow-angle fluctuations. Maximum deviations of aerodynamic loss, area-averaged film-cooling effectiveness, and area-averaged Nusselt number reach 0.33%, 45%, and 5.0%. Global sensitivity analysis identifies inlet flow angle as the most significant parameter; positive incidence harms aerodynamic performance and endwall thermal management.
