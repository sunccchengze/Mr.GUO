## 二、摘要原文（English，逐字摘录，未改写）

> To clarify the influence of various design variables on turbine stage performance in a high-dimensional design space, SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP), a data mining method, is applied to the 93-dimensional design space of the first-stage of high-pressure turbine of the GE-E3 engine. Besides the global performance analysis based on scalar output (0-dimension data), advanced methods are developed to analyze the impact of significant variables on spanwise performance distributions (1-dimension data) and meridional physical field contours (2-dimension data). These methods enable the localization and visualization of specific physical regions and parameters within the turbine stage that are influenced by the significant design variables improving overall performance. Leveraging SHAP’s local interpretability, typical samples are selected for attribution analysis to reveal the contribution of individual design variables to performance variations. The results show that significant variables affecting stage efficiency include effective outlet angles, three-dimensional stacking parameters of stator, and spline control points near the suction-side leading edge. Based on the data mining of the GE-E3 dataset, design guidelines are summarized.
