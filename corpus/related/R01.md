## 二、摘要（英文，摘自分叉归档的出版商公开页）

The Kennedy–O’Hagan Bayesian multi-fidelity surrogate decomposes a high-fidelity function into a scaled low-fidelity model plus a discrepancy function. The scale factor is commonly tuned by maximum likelihood estimation, but this can produce poor accuracy because it overemphasizes discrepancy variation and ignores function waviness. The paper selects the scale factor by minimizing posterior variance of the discrepancy. Tests on one- and high-dimensional functions and a turbine-blade design problem show accuracy comparable to or better than MLE and greater robustness; an endwall-cooling-layout optimization further validates the approach.
