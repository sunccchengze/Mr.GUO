## 二、摘要原文（English，逐字摘录自 SPIE 出版商页面）

> Traditional CFD and 1D/quasi-3D through-flow programs suffer from bottlenecks of "being slow" and "being inaccurate" in turbomachinery design. The fourth paradigm of AI offers a new direction, yet purely data-driven models face issues such as limited samples and poor generalization. To solve the above problems, this paper proposes a panoramic prediction model based on physics-informed machine learning, taking a high-pressure turbine stage as the research object which includes the features of the Navier–Stokes equations. The model first predicts basic variables (temperature, pressure, density, velocity) and then comprehensively evaluates meridional-plane performance parameters. A dataset is constructed using the GE-E3 high-pressure stage, and a Transformer-augmented neural operator network is employed for model building and testing. It shows that the model can not only predict overall performance indicators (e.g., power, efficiency, mass flow rate) but also spanwise distributions of key parameters (e.g., exit flow angle, stage reaction) at the millisecond level, with relative errors of all parameters below 1%, verifying the effectiveness of the proposed physics-informed flow field prediction model.

**摘要中译**：传统 CFD 与一维/准三维通流程序在叶轮机设计中存在"慢"与"不准"两大瓶颈；AI 第四范式提供了新方向，但纯数据驱动模型面临样本有限、泛化差的问题。为此，本文以**高压涡轮级**为对象，提出一种基于物理信息机器学习的全景预测模型，模型中包含 Navier–Stokes 方程的特征。模型**先预测基本变量（温度、压力、密度、速度），再综合评估子午面性能参数**。数据集基于 **GE-E3 高压级**构建，采用 **Transformer 增强的神经算子网络**建模与测试。结果表明，模型不仅能预测总体性能指标（功率、效率、流量），还能预测关键参数的展向分布（出口气流角、级反动度），达到**毫秒级**，且**所有参数相对误差低于 1%**。

## 三、可写入白皮书的关键事实（逐条有据，均出自摘要）

1. **研究对象**：高压涡轮级；数据集基于 **GE-E3 高压级**。
2. **物理增强的形式（摘要级）**：模型"包含 N-S 方程的特征"，并采用**两段式**——先预测 T、p、ρ、v 四类基本场，再由基本场综合计算子午面性能参数（与论文 16 TNO 的"输出侧注入"同构）。
3. **网络类型**：Transformer 增强的神经算子。
4. **输出**：总体指标（功率、效率、流量）+ 展向分布（出口气流角、级反动度）。
5. **速度与精度口径**：毫秒级推理；**所有参数相对误差 < 1%**。
